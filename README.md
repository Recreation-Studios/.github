# Recreation Studios

> *"Bringing back the golden days? Or just missing our childhood?"*

---

### Who are we?
Honestly, we are just a bunch of developers trying to figure out how classic Minecraft .jar files work; preserving that specific, unrepeatable nostalgia.

### What are we breaking... uh, building?
*   **Classic Revivals:** Bringing back old official .jar Minecraft versions, mods, and launchers with fixes to run again!
*   **Open Source Chaos:** Everything we do is open for the community to break, fix, and improve.
